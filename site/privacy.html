Privacy Policy

Last updated 11 November 2026

Middle C is made by Liskov Labs. The short version: the app doesn't collect any data about you, and nothing you do in it leaves your device.

What the app collects

Nothing. Middle C has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. It doesn't send data to Liskov Labs or to anyone else, and it doesn't need an internet connection to work.

The microphone

If you turn on Listen to my piano when no MIDI keyboard is connected in Settings, Middle C uses the microphone to hear which notes you play. The sound is analysed on your device as you play. It is never recorded, saved or sent anywhere. The app only asks for microphone access when you turn this setting on, and you can turn it off at any time, in the app or in your device's Settings.

MIDI keyboards

When a keyboard is connected, Middle C reads the notes it sends so that it can follow your playing. These notes are used while you practise and are not stored or shared.

What's stored on your device

Your progress through the lessons, your stars and your settings are saved on the device you use, using the system's standard app storage. They stay there. Deleting the app deletes them.

This website

Everything above is about the app. This website is different: if you accept cookies when you first visit, it uses Google Tag Manager and Google Analytics to count visits and see which pages people read. Google may then set cookies and receive your IP address, browser details and the pages you visit. If you decline, none of this is loaded and the site works the same. This data is not linked to the app or to anything you do in it.

You can change your choice at any time with the Cookie settings link at the bottom of every page. See how Google uses this data.

Children

Middle C is suitable for learners of any age. Because it collects no personal information, it collects none from children either.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. If the app ever started collecting data, this page would say so before it happened.

Contact

Questions about privacy are welcome. See the support page for how to get in touch.
