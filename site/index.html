Get Middle C See how it works

For Mac, iPad and iPhone. Works with USB-MIDI keyboards, acoustic pianos, or no keyboard at all.

Bring any keyboard

It hears you, however you play.

Middle C follows every note you play and tells you straight away whether it was the right one.

USB-MIDI keyboard

Plug a digital keyboard into your Mac, iPad or iPhone and switch it on. It's recognised straight away, with no driver to install. Written with the Casio CT-S300 in mind.

Acoustic piano

No MIDI port? Turn on the microphone and Middle C listens to your piano and works out which keys you're pressing. The sound is analysed on your device and never recorded.

No keyboard yet

Start right away on the 61-key on-screen keyboard. It's multi-touch on iPad and iPhone, and on a Mac the A S D F G H J K row of your computer keyboard plays the white keys.

Two ways to practise

Practice

The music waits for you.

Take as long as you need. The next key lights up on the keyboard, with the finger to use, and the music only moves on when you press the right one.

  • Key hints and finger numbers on the keyboard
  • Letter names in the note heads while you're learning to read
  • A progress bar for every exercise, and stars for every lesson
In time

A metronome, a moving staff, and an honest ear.

A measure of clicks counts you in, the staff scrolls past at a steady beat, and every note you play is marked as you play it.

Play anything and watch it appear on the treble and bass staves, with the name of each note. It's a quick way to learn where the notes sit on the staff.

Questions

Good to know

Which keyboards work with Middle C?

Any keyboard or digital piano with a USB port that sends MIDI. Most do, and need no driver. The app was written with the Casio CT-S300 in mind, and its 61-key layout (C2 to C7) matches that keyboard.

How do I connect a keyboard to an iPad or iPhone?

Plug it into the USB-C port with a USB cable, or use a Lightning to USB adapter on older devices. It works the same way as on a Mac.

I have an acoustic piano. Can I still use it?

Yes. Turn on the microphone in Settings and Middle C listens to your piano to work out which notes you play. It picks out chords too, and it works best in a quiet room.

Do I need to read music already?

No. The course starts at the very beginning, finding Middle C, and teaches you to read the staff along the way. Letter names in the note heads help you until you don't need them.

Does it need an internet connection or an account?

No. Everything runs on your device, and there's nothing to sign up for.

What does it sound like on iPhone and iPad?

On a Mac you hear the piano from the sound bank built into macOS. iOS has no built-in piano sound, so the on-screen keys play a plain tone. With a keyboard connected you hear the keyboard's own speakers.
