Support

Something not working, or a question about Middle C? Start with the answers below. If they don't help, get in touch.

Report a problem or ask a question

Open an issue on GitHub. Say which device and system version you use, which keyboard (if any), and what happened. A screenshot helps.

My keyboard isn't recognised

  • Check that the keyboard is switched on and connected with a USB data cable. Some cables only carry power.
  • On iPhone and iPad, connect to the USB-C port, or use Apple's Lightning to USB Camera Adapter on older devices.
  • Unplug the keyboard and plug it in again. Middle C picks up keyboards as they're connected; there's no driver to install.
  • The status at the bottom of the sidebar (or the top of a lesson) shows the keyboard's name once it's connected.

The microphone doesn't hear my notes

  • Turn on Listen to my piano in Middle C's Settings, and allow microphone access when asked.
  • If you said no earlier, allow it in the system Settings: on Mac, under Privacy & Security → Microphone; on iPhone and iPad, under Settings → Middle C.
  • Put the device near the piano, and practise somewhere quiet. Other music or talking can confuse it.

Notes are marked late in in-time mode

  • Bluetooth headphones and speakers add a delay to the metronome, so you hear the beat later than it happens. Use the device's own speaker or wired headphones.
  • Slow the song down to 60% or 80% until it feels comfortable.

There's no piano sound on iPhone or iPad

iOS has no built-in piano sound, so the on-screen keys play a plain tone. Check that the ringer switch isn't on silent. With a keyboard connected, you hear the keyboard's own sound.

Requirements

  • Mac: macOS 13 Ventura or later
  • iPhone and iPad: iOS / iPadOS 16 or later
