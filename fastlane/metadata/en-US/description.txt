Learn to play the piano, starting from Middle C.

Plug in your keyboard, follow short lessons, and Middle C hears every note you play. It waits while you find the keys, then counts you in when you're ready to play in time.

BRING ANY KEYBOARD
• USB-MIDI keyboard: plug a digital keyboard into your iPhone, iPad or Mac and switch it on. It's recognised straight away, with no driver to install.
• Acoustic piano: no MIDI port? Turn on the microphone and Middle C listens to your piano and works out which keys you press. The sound is analysed on your device and never recorded.
• No keyboard yet: start right away on the 61-key on-screen keyboard. It's multi-touch on iPhone and iPad, and on a Mac your computer keyboard plays the white keys.

TWO WAYS TO PRACTISE
• Practice: the music waits for you. The next key lights up, with the finger to use, and the music only moves on when you press the right one.
• In time: a measure of clicks counts you in, the staff scrolls past at a steady beat, and every note is marked on the beat, early, late or missed.

A COURSE FOR BEGINNERS
8 units and 27 short lessons take you from finding Middle C to playing a tune with chords in your left hand. Each lesson takes a few minutes and builds on the last:
• Meet the Keys
• Feel the Beat
• Read the Staff
• The Left Hand
• Hands Together
• Quicker Notes
• Beyond Five Notes
• Chords

18 SONGS AT YOUR OWN SPEED
Traditional and classical favourites, from Hot Cross Buns and Ode to Joy to Amazing Grace, Minuet in G and the opening of Für Elise. Practise with the right hand, the left hand or both, at 60%, 80% or full speed.

FREE PLAY
Play anything and watch it appear on the treble and bass staves, with the name of each note. A quick way to learn where the notes sit on the staff.

MADE FOR EACH DEVICE
• Mac: the whole 61-key keyboard on screen, and a real piano sound.
• iPad: prop it on the music stand and plug your keyboard into the USB port.
• iPhone: upright or on its side. The keyboard shows the part the music needs.
Get it once and use it on all three.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No account, no ads, no tracking. Middle C collects no data and works without an internet connection. Your progress stays on your device.
